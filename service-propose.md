## Positioning Tip

Given your background in full-stack development, backend architecture, DDD, DevOps, Docker, AI integrations, and building production systems, I'd avoid marketing yourself as a generic "Full-Stack Developer."

A stronger headline is:

> **I help startups and businesses design, build, and scale reliable web products.**

That positions you as someone who solves business and engineering problems—not just someone who writes code.
